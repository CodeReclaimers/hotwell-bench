## C2: windowed detectability sweep (PLAN-bench-0.2.md §3.1)

**What was built:** `scripts/window_sweep.py`, which writes the generated module
`src/hotwell_bench/window_detectability.py` (THRESHOLDS_V020, SENSOR_BIAS_THRESHOLD,
SENSOR_NOISE_THRESHOLD) and raw results to `out/window_sweep/results.json` (gitignored).
- Setup is G-B2's: AB + 3E, flat 60 MW, no operator, seed 42, step at 300 s, run to
  3600 s. Every item is labelled by `label_runs(..., preset=P02)`.
- Grid: 2^(−k/2), k = −2…10 (2× down to 1/32×) of the 0.1 threshold (mechanism rows
  with a "swept" source) or of D_i (sensor bias, both signs). noise_increase factors
  1.05, 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.5, 2, 3.
- Threshold of a row = smallest grid value such that it and every larger one are
  detected. Bias takes the larger of the two signs. "assumed: X" rows take X's value.
- Drift is to take the bias threshold (a drift ends at its max bias); not in the module
  yet, since the sampler wiring is C3.
- *Not chosen:* a longer horizon than 1 h. It keeps the 0.1 definition, but it binds on
  tube_leak (detected at 2964 s at the threshold). Flagged to Alan.

**Run:** `python -u scripts/window_sweep.py --workers 30`: `949 items: {'mechanism': 91,
'bias': 676, 'noise': 182}`, `runs done in 2054 s`, results sha256 `7f2d038b6ce40152…`.
A second run after reformatting the generator (cache hits, `runs done in 2 s`) gave
identical tables (`diff` empty).

**Attribution (thresholds recomputed from results.json per rule subset):**

**G-B2 style cross-check:**
- On the 14 grid points that coincide with hotwell's sweep rows, the P02 delay and
  instrument equal hotwell's exactly. For example, tube_leak 0.1: hotwell 689
  hotwell_level, P02 689 level/hotwell_level. Undetectable rows (frv/lcv 0.01, firing
  0.005) stay undetected.
- The stuck and variance rules fire on no mechanism row (`mechanism rows with
  stuck/variance hits: []`).

**Findings needing decisions (diagnosed, not fixed):**
1. *Variance rule on idle instruments.* cp2_current, fp2_current, makeup_flow and
   makeup_valve_pos have bias thresholds of 0.0625–0.125 D_i, below 1 LSB.
   - The detecting rule is variance. Example: makeup_valve_pos with bias 0.000625 (LSB
     0.005). Readings sit on {0, 0.005, 0.01}, so the reading is clipped at 0 and
     quantized. The bias shifts the quantized distribution: mean F−T 0.00035, var F/T
     1.56.
   - This is real in the data but sub-LSB. These sources would make C3 sample tiny
     biases on idle instruments.
2. *Frequency bias is never detected* (any rule, up to 2 D_i = 0.04 Hz). Mean F−T on
   frequency is 0.0031 for a 0.04 bias, so the control loop compensates the reading. The
   module records None.
3. *noise_increase:* threshold 1.3 on 23 instruments, frequency included (1.2 not detected). fw_valve_pos
   and da_lcv_pos: 2; throttle_pos: 1.5.
