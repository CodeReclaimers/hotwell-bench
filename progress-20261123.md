**Why these.** Round 1 was too easy (CUSUM fault detection 1.000; probes no lower than
on 0.1). Smaller magnitudes are the direct lever. Decoys cost CUSUM on null days in
round 1, so more of them.

**A conflict in the §0 target, flagged to Alan (not resolved).** The labeller accepts a
fault only if some 600 s window clears the null-noise threshold. CUSUM integrates over
hours, so it is more sensitive than the acceptance bar. Any accepted fault may then be
one CUSUM detects, and "CUSUM fault-day detection well below 0.9" may be unreachable
by knobs alone. This is reasoning, not a measurement; round 2 tests it.

**Pairing.** The same masters serve round 2 (dev 2026112202, CUSUM training
2026112203), so items stay paired across rounds. Decoy presence is drawn as
`u_has < p_decoy` from an attempt-independent stream, so every round-1 decoy item keeps
its decoys and some items gain them.
