hours, so it is more sensitive than the acceptance bar. Any accepted fault may then be

by knobs alone. This is reasoning, not a measurement; round 2 tests it.

`u_has < p_decoy` from an attempt-independent stream, so every round-1 decoy item keeps
its decoys and some items gain them.

observable; 0.75–1, 15 of 24; 1 and above, 31 of 34. The labeller's acceptance bar sits at

the §7 knobs** while acceptance requires windowed observability. This settles the
conflict flagged before round 2 by measurement. It is Alan's decision whether to restate
