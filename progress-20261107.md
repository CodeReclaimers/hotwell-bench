**Findings.**
1. **The twin-difference onset criterion is structurally blind to variance and
   dynamics changes** (stuck, noise_increase, partly lag). Stuck sensors on noisy
   instruments would all be dropped as "unobservable". Recorded in plan §4.1 as a required
   B3 change: per-kind statistics plus a seeded gate test each.
2. **Floor bias also affects `makeup_valve_pos`** (reads 0–0.005, spikes to 0.01 shut), not
   only `makeup_flow`. task.md (B4) must cover every floor-resting instrument, including
   idle pump currents.
3. **Makeup pulses on the hotwell 1 mm limit cycle** make leaks very visible once makeup
   starts: up to ~1 kg/s spikes for a 0.063 kg/s leak. B3 difficulty strata should
   account for it.
4. **Stuck firing tripped the plant in 16 min at flat 60 MW** (AB + 3E). Hotwell's 1 h
   sweep measured detection at 115 s for this fault but doesn't report trips, so whether
   a trip in the hour is typical is unknown. B3 counts trips per category.
